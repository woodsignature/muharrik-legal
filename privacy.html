MuharrikStudio Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

MuharrikStudio is a desktop application that helps creators generate, manage, and publish original video content to supported social media platforms.

1. Information We Collect

MuharrikStudio may process information required to connect authorized social media accounts and provide publishing functionality.

  • Basic account information provided by connected platforms.
  • Authentication tokens required to authorize API access.
  • Video files selected or generated by the user.
  • Video titles, descriptions, hashtags, and publishing settings.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide the features requested by the user, including connecting social media accounts, preparing content, and publishing authorized content through official platform APIs.

3. TikTok

MuharrikStudio may use TikTok's official APIs to allow authorized users to connect their TikTok account and publish content through supported TikTok publishing features.

4. Data Sharing

MuharrikStudio does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to a connected social media platform when necessary to provide a feature explicitly requested by the user.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect authentication information and data processed by the application.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only as necessary to provide the application's features or comply with legal and security requirements.

7. User Control

Users can disconnect authorized social media accounts and stop using publishing features at any time.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: muharrikstudio@gmail.com
