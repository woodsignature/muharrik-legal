MuharrikStudio Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

MuharrikStudio ("MuharrikStudio", "we", "our", or "us") is a desktop application designed to help creators generate, manage, and publish their original video content to connected social media platforms.

1. Information We Collect

MuharrikStudio may process information necessary to connect your social media accounts and provide publishing functionality.

This may include:

  • Account identifiers provided by connected platforms.
  • Authentication tokens required to authorize publishing.
  • Basic profile information provided by the connected platform.
  • Video files selected or generated by the user.
  • Video titles, descriptions, hashtags, and other publishing metadata.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only to provide the features requested by the user, including connecting social media accounts, generating content, preparing publishing information, and publishing content to platforms authorized by the user.

3. Social Media Platforms

When you connect a social media account, MuharrikStudio uses the applicable platform's official APIs and authorization mechanisms.

For TikTok, MuharrikStudio uses TikTok's official developer APIs to allow authorized users to publish content to their TikTok account.

4. User Control

Users control which social media accounts they connect and which content they choose to publish. MuharrikStudio does not publish content to a connected account without the required authorization and user consent.

5. Data Sharing

MuharrikStudio does not sell personal information.

Information is transmitted to third-party platforms only when necessary to provide a feature requested by the user, such as publishing content to a connected social media account.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect authentication credentials, access tokens, and other information processed by the application.

Authentication credentials and access tokens are not intentionally disclosed to unrelated third parties.

7. Data Retention

MuharrikStudio retains information only for as long as necessary to provide its features or as required for legitimate operational, legal, or security purposes.

Users may disconnect their social media accounts at any time through the available account controls or the relevant platform.

8. Children's Privacy

MuharrikStudio is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to independently provide consent to data processing.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us through the official MuharrikStudio contact information provided on our website.
